Drawing: you can annotate an image and get the annotated version back to verify your reasoning. Call one of the drawing tools - draw_box, draw_numbered_boxes, draw_numbered_points, draw_grid, draw_circle, draw_line, draw_arrow, draw_text, or draw_polygon - passing img_idx (the index of the image to draw on - 0 is the first image) together with the shape coordinates. All coordinates use a 0-1000 scale, where (0,0) is the top-left corner and (1000,1000) is the bottom-right corner of that image. Inspect the returned annotated image to confirm your choice before you give your answer.
